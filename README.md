# Drawing the Unseen

A 90-minute Architect Dreamin workshop on diagramming AI in a Salesforce architecture. Groups of five work the fictional Meridian Mobility Group brief: pick AI capabilities under a 12-point cap, draw a solution architecture and an AI data flow with a deliberately incomplete notation kit, and defend the result to one executive on the review board.

## Live site

The whole kit is published as a single self-contained page: https://rammc.github.io/drawing-the-unseen/

## What's inside

- `index.html` is the workshop hub. One self-contained page that embeds every diagram and renders every document, and works offline.
- `diagrams/` holds the notation kit and the eight worked diagrams as editable SVGs alongside PNG exports.
- `docs/` holds the source documents: the scenario (`mmg-scenario.md`), the technology card deck (`mmg-tech-card-deck.md`), and the facilitator playbook (`facilitator-playbook.md`). `docs/archive/` keeps the earlier playbook for reference.
- `materials/` holds the print-ready session kit: `drawing-the-unseen-technology-cards.docx` (the A4 card deck), `drawing-the-unseen-judge-cards.pdf` and `.docx` (the A5 judge cards, one per executive).
- `prompts/` holds a ready prompt for building the opening slides.

## What to print

Per table of five:
- One landscape worksheet of the MMG capability map (`diagrams/diagram-1-capability-map.png` or `.svg`).
- One technology card deck cut from `materials/drawing-the-unseen-technology-cards.docx`.
- Markers in three colours.

Per person:
- One notation kit cheat sheet (`diagrams/ai-notation-kit.png` or `.svg`).

Per facilitator playing the board:
- One A5 judge card per executive from `materials/drawing-the-unseen-judge-cards.pdf` (Helena, Marcus, Priya).

## How to run it

Open `docs/facilitator-playbook.md` (or scroll to the Playbook section on the live site). It covers timing, the scenario intro, the notation walk-through, the point model, the review board, and the close.

## A note on the scenario

Meridian Mobility Group is a fictional company built for this workshop. Any resemblance to a real organisation is coincidental.

## Status

The notation kit is version 0.1 and deliberately incomplete. The vocabulary the room invents during the session is the path toward version 1.0.
